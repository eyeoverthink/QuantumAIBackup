# Specification for UNFRAMEABLE

## Field of the Invention

This invention relates to systems and methods for digital identity protection using phi-harmonic masking, quantum noise signature embedding, and blockchain-linked immutable chains.

## Background of the Invention

The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence and quantum computing presents significant threats to conventional cryptographic systems and biometric identity systems. Deepfakes and data leaks are increasingly common, requiring novel identity protection solutions.

## Summary of the Invention

The UNFRAMEABLE system protects user identity through:

1. φ-Mask: Applies a phi-transformed distortion of original biometric data.
2. ψ-Lock: Uses a quantum-derived signature from the φ-mask, simulating quantum resistance.
3. ChainLock: Anchors the resulting ID to a RealityChain with cryptographic hashing and timestamping.

Together, this architecture forms a three-tiered, tamper-evident, mathematically robust identity protection protocol that resists forgery, cloning, and spoofing.

## Detailed Description

The system operates by accepting real biometric input (e.g., facial data), transforming it using a phi-harmonic function (phi\_transform), embedding quantum noise (quantum\_noise), and deriving a digital signature (quantum\_signature). This locked identity is embedded into a blockchain-inspired structure (RealityChain) which timestamps and verifies the consistency of the ID over time.

Verification requires resubmission of the real input, which is reprocessed and compared against the chain's original hash-linked data structure. If coherence and signature match, identity is confirmed.

# Claims

1. A system for identity protection comprising:

   * a phi-transform layer configured to mask biometric input;
   * a quantum noise generator adding randomized data to the phi-mask;
   * a signature generator forming a quantum-derived hash of the masked identity;
   * and a cryptographic blockchain-inspired RealityChain storing said identity and signature.

2. The system of claim 1 wherein the phi-transform layer uses irrational number transformations and bitwise inversions.

3. The system of claim 1 wherein the quantum noise is dynamically generated per identity instance to ensure uniqueness.

4. The system of claim 1 wherein the RealityChain comprises timestamped, tamper-evident records verified through hash digest comparison.

5. The system of claim 1 wherein said system resists deepfake identity substitution and traditional biometric spoofing.

# Abstract (Reformatted for USPTO Filing)

A digital identity protection system leveraging phi-harmonic transformation, simulated quantum signature embedding, and cryptographic chaining. The system masks real biometric inputs with mathematically derived φ-patterns and noise, producing tamper-proof, blockchain-anchored identity hashes. Designed for post-quantum resilience, it provides strong verification against spoofing, deepfakes, and forgery.
