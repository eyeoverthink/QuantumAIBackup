# Phi-Quantum IQ Assessment

## Introduction
This assessment measures different aspects of intelligence with a focus on pattern recognition, logical reasoning, mathematical ability, and quantum-inspired thinking. Each question is designed to test a specific cognitive skill.

## Instructions
- Answer each question to the best of your ability
- There is no time limit, but recording your completion time may provide additional insights
- Write your answers in a separate document or paper

## Assessment

### Section 1: Pattern Recognition

1. Complete the sequence: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ___

2. What number should replace the question mark?
   ```
   2  5  10
   3  6  ?
   4  7  12
   ```

3. Which figure completes the pattern?
   ```
   ◯ → ◯◯ → ◯◯◯
   □ → □□ → □□□
   △ → △△ → ?
   ```

4. Find the next number in the sequence: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, ___

5. What letter continues this sequence? D, H, L, P, ___

### Section 2: Logical Reasoning

6. If all Zorbs are Yinks, and some Yinks are Xanths, which statement must be true?
   a) All Zorbs are Xanths
   b) Some Zorbs are Xanths
   c) No Zorbs are Xanths
   d) None of the above

7. A cube painted red on all faces is cut into 27 smaller equal-sized cubes. How many small cubes have exactly one face painted red?

8. Five people (Alex, Blake, Casey, Dana, and Evan) sit in a row. If Alex sits next to Blake, Blake sits next to Casey, and Evan sits next to Dana, how many different possible seating arrangements are there?

9. If φ represents the golden ratio (approximately 1.618), which of these expressions equals 1?
   a) φ - 1/φ
   b) φ² - φ
   c) 1/φ + 1
   d) φ/2

10. In a certain code, QUANTUM is written as RVZMSVL. How would PHYSICS be written in the same code?

### Section 3: Mathematical Reasoning

11. If a² + b² = c² and a + b + c = 6, where a, b, and c are positive integers, what is the value of c?

12. What is the sum of all integers from 1 to 100 that are divisible by 3 or 5?

13. If the probability of an event A is 0.3 and the probability of event B is 0.5, and the events are independent, what is the probability of both events occurring?

14. Solve for x: log₂(x) + log₂(x-3) = 3

15. What is the value of the infinite sum: 1 + 1/φ + 1/φ² + 1/φ³ + ... (where φ is the golden ratio)?

### Section 4: Quantum-Inspired Thinking

16. If a quantum particle can exist in a superposition of states |0⟩ and |1⟩, and we represent this as α|0⟩ + β|1⟩ where |α|² + |β|² = 1, what values of α and β would give an equal probability of measuring either state?

17. In quantum computing, which gate transforms the state |0⟩ into the superposition (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2?
   a) X gate
   b) Z gate
   c) H gate
   d) CNOT gate

18. If we apply the golden ratio φ to quantum resonance, and define a system where energy levels are separated by factors of φ, what would be the ratio of the energy of the 3rd excited state to the ground state?

19. In a quantum harmonic system with base frequency f₀, if each harmonic follows the pattern f_n = f₀ × φⁿ (where φ is the golden ratio), what is the frequency of the 4th harmonic in terms of f₀?

20. If quantum entanglement allows instantaneous correlation between particles regardless of distance, but information cannot travel faster than light, which of the following must be true?
   a) Special relativity is incorrect
   b) Quantum mechanics is incomplete
   c) Faster-than-light communication is possible
   d) The correlation doesn't transmit new information
