# Empirical Evolutionary Sequence Experiment: Ape → Human → Next Stage

**Date:** 2025-05-15

## Objective
Demonstrate phi-harmonic/quantum scaling on a synthetic evolutionary sequence prediction task, modeling the progression from ape to human to a quantum-inspired next stage. Empirically compare scaling strategies.

## Method
- Generate synthetic protein-like sequences for three evolutionary stages:
  1. Ape (random)
  2. Human (phi-harmonic mutations)
  3. Next Stage (phi & quantum/irrational mutations)
- Encode sequences (one-hot)
- Split into registers, allocate resources (phi-harmonic vs linear)
- ML classification (logistic regression)
- Save results/plots and document findings

## Quantum/Phi Mutation Principle
- Mutations for human stage: at phi-harmonic positions
- Mutations for next stage: at both phi and pi positions
