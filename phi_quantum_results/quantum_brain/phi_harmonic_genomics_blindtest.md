# Phi-Harmonic Genomics Blind Test

## Purpose
Conducts a blind, unsupervised analysis of real breast cancer data using phi-harmonic, quantum, and hyperdimensional methods. Compares system-discovered patterns to true clinical outcomes for scientific validation.

## Data Source
- [breast-cancer.csv](https://github.com/datasets/breast-cancer/blob/main/data/breast-cancer.csv)
- Features used: age, tumor-size, invaded nodes, degree of malignancy
- Outcome: recurrence-events vs. false-recurrence-events

## Blind Test Workflow
1. **Preprocessing:** Categorical features encoded numerically; class labels hidden during analysis.
2. **Phi-Harmonic Analysis:**
   - Spiral mapping (first sample)
   - Hyperdimensional projections (PCA, t-SNE)
   - Quantum resonance map (3D PCA)
3. **Ground Truth Comparison:**
   - After unsupervised analysis, clusters/patterns are compared to recurrence outcomes.
   - Visualizes true recurrence vs. non-recurrence on the same PCA projection.

## Results & Insights
- The phi-harmonic system can reveal structure and clustering in real clinical data without access to labels.
- Comparison to ground truth enables quantitative and qualitative validation of discovered patterns.
- Further tuning (feature engineering, more dimensions, phi-harmonic metrics) can enhance separation and insight.
